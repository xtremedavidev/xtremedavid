01 — Impulse capture

27 trigger pairs feel every axle.

As a vehicle brakes across the 12 × 3 m REPS plate, hydraulic cylinders under each trigger turn its momentum into vertical force. The heavier the axle, the more it gives back.

Plate
12 m × 3 m
Road slabs

02 — Hydraulic transmission

Force travels. Fragile parts don’t.

Each impulse moves through sealed, closed-loop lines to a central Energy Converter Module beside the road — so the sensitive hardware never sits under traffic.

Lines
closed loop, sealed
Maintenance
off the carriageway
03 — Frictionless conversion

A shaft that floats on magnets.

Permanent magnetic bearings levitate the generator shaft. No contact, no wear — and more than 90% of the captured energy converted to electricity.
